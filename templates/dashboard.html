Interpretation of the Dashboard

The data shows a clear warming trend. Pakistan's yearly average temperature has risen over the long term, and the trained prediction model estimates it will reach about 22.07 C by 2050. Between 2001 and 2013, 41 months were abnormally hot (1.5 C or more above their 1951 to 1980 normal), and these months are listed as alerts by the system. The strongest single anomaly was March 2010, at +4.31 C above normal. The correlation between CO2 and global temperature is 0.9655, a very strong positive relation, which supports the warming trend shown by the models.
