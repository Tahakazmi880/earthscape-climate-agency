Help and FAQ

User Guide

Dashboard: The home page. It shows the key figures of the project, the interpretation of the results, the Pakistan temperature trend with the model forecast, and the anomaly alerts per year.

Data Explorer: Select any of the 242 countries to see its yearly average temperature chart with its record range, average, lowest and highest values.

Predictions: Shows the trained models and their test results. Use the Live Prediction Tool to enter any future year (2014 to 2100) and get the model's prediction immediately.

Alerts: Lists the anomaly alerts found in the data. Use the Live Anomaly Checker to test any month: enter its average temperature and the system compares it with the 1951 to 1980 baseline and tells you whether an alert would be raised.

Correlation: Shows the relation between CO2 and global temperature as a scatter chart. The correlation value is 0.9655.

Upload Data: Upload your own climate CSV file (dt and AverageTemperature columns). The system processes it live and draws the yearly chart.

Feedback and Support: Send feedback, report an issue, or ask for support. Administrators can read all submissions on the Admin page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the data come from?
The Berkeley Earth "Climate Change: Earth Surface Temperature Data" dataset (Kaggle), which contains measured land temperature records from weather stations worldwide, plus NOAA CO2 data for 1959 to 2025.

What is an anomaly alert?
A month is flagged when its average temperature is 1.5 C or more above its baseline, where the baseline is the 1951 to 1980 average of the same month. Alerts are graded Moderate, High and Severe.

How accurate is the prediction model?
Model 2 (trained on 1950 to 2000 data) has a test RMSE of 0.52 C on the years 2001 to 2013. It is a trend model: it shows the long term direction, not exact yearly values.

Why does the country data end in 2013?
The Berkeley Earth country dataset's records end in 2013. The CO2 companion data runs to 2025.

Who can see the Admin page?
Only users with the Administrator role. Analysts are denied access, as defined in the access control design of the project.

How do I create an account?
On the Sign In page, open "Create an account". Fill in your full name, email address and a username, choose a password, and your account is created at once with the Analyst role. You are signed in straight away and can explore the dashboard, predictions, alerts and live weather.

I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the Sign In page, open "Forgot password?", type your username and press the button. The system shows you a recovery code that stays valid for 30 minutes. Type the code with your new password and you can sign in again. If the code expires, the Administrator can also reset your password from the Admin page.
