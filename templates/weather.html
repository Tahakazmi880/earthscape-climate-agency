Weather: Today, Past Dates, and Averages

Write the name of any city of any country, then press a button. You can see the weather of right now, of one old date, or the average of many days. All data is free and comes from Open-Meteo, a weather service with records of many past years.

Examples: Karachi, Lahore, Dubai, London, New York.

1Weather right now

See the weather of your city at this moment.

2Weather on one day

Pick any date, old or new. You will see that full day. You can also see all 24 hours of that day, one by one.

3Average between two dates

Pick a first date and a last date. You will get the average temperature and the total rain of all those days, a graph, and a table of every day.

4Compare two cities

Write a second city. Both cities are checked on the same dates of Box 3, and you can see which city was hotter and how much rain each city got.

5One full year, month by month

Write a year. You will see all 12 months of that year, one row for every month.
