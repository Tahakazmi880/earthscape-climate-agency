Weather of Any City in the World

This page answers 5 simple questions. Write your city name, pick a box below, and press its button. Every box has an example so you can see what will happen before you press anything. The dates are already filled with a working example, so you can also just press a button and watch.

Examples: Karachi, Lahore, Dubai, London, New York.

1How is the weather right now?

Example: city is Karachi. Press the button and you see the temperature of this minute, the sky, the wind, and the water in the air.

2How was the weather on one day?

Example: the date below is 15 June 2024. Press "Show this day" and you see that full day: how hot, how cold, how much rain. Press "Show 24 hours" and you see that same day hour by hour, from midnight to midnight.

3How were many days together?

Example: the dates below are 1 June 2024 to 30 June 2024, the full month of June. You get one average temperature for the whole month and the total rain of the whole month, plus a graph and every day in a table. Real answer for Karachi: that June was 31.09 C on average and only 12.2 mm rain fell in the full month.

4Which city was hotter?

Example: your city is Karachi and the second city is Dubai. Both are checked on the same dates of Box 3. You see both cities side by side, and one clear line tells you which city was hotter and by how much.

5How was one full year?

Example: the year below is 2024. You see all 12 months in one table, January to December. Real answer for Karachi 2024: July was the hottest month (31.43 C average) and August had the most rain (147.8 mm).
