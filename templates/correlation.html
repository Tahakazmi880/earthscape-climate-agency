Correlation Analysis: CO2 and Temperature

This graph is of the whole world, not one country. Every dot is one year, from 1959 to 2025. Move your mouse on a dot: it tells you that year, how much CO2 was in the air, and how hot the world was.

The link between CO2 and heat is 0.9655. The highest link is 1, so 0.9655 means a very strong link: more CO2, more heat. As CO2 went up, the world got hotter. That is what this page shows.
