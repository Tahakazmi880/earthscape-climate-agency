Correlation Analysis: CO2 and Temperature

Whose data is this? This graph is not for one country. It uses whole-world (global) data: the CO2 of the whole Earth's air (NOAA record) and the average land temperature of the whole world, every year from 1959 to 2025. Each dot is one year. Move your mouse over a dot and a box shows that year, its CO2 and its temperature.

The Pearson correlation between yearly CO2 levels and global land temperature (1959 to 2025) is 0.9655. A value this close to 1 means a very strong positive relation: as CO2 increased, temperature increased with it.
